**Job Market Paper**

**With [Ken Troske](https://gatton.uky.edu/faculty-research/faculty/troske-kenneth) ([University of Kentucky](https://www.uky.edu))**

[Download the draft (PDF)](/files/Linkhorn_JMP.pdf)

This paper exploits cross-state variation in UI benefit duration cuts enacted between 2016 and 2025 to estimate the causal effects of reduced UI generosity on labor market outcomes. Using administrative data, we examine how reduced benefits alter re-employment, the timing and quality of re-employment matches, earnings trajectories, and re-entry wages for displaced workers.
