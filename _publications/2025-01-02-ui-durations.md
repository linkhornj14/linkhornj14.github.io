**With Ken Troske (University of Kentucky)**

This paper exploits cross-state variation in UI benefit duration cuts enacted between 2011 and 2016 to estimate the causal effects of reduced UI generosity on labor market outcomes. Using administrative data, we examine how shorter benefit windows alter job search behavior, the timing and quality of re-employment matches, and longer-run earnings trajectories for displaced workers.
