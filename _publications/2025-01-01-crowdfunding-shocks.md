This paper examines whether households use online crowdfunding platforms as informal insurance against adverse economic shocks. I find that access to federal unemployment benefits during high unemployment leads to reduced campaign creation on [GoFundMe](https://www.gofundme.com), suggesting that crowdfunding serves a genuine consumption-smoothing tool — particularly for households with limited access to formal insurance.

*Presented at the [Southern Economic Association 94th Annual Meeting](https://southerneconomic.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2024-SEA-Program.pdf) (2024).*
