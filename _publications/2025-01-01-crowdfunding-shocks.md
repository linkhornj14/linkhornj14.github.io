This paper examines whether households use online crowdfunding platforms as informal insurance against adverse economic shocks. I find that job loss and unexpected medical expenses are significant predictors of campaign creation on GoFundMe, suggesting that crowdfunding serves a genuine consumption-smoothing role — particularly for households with limited access to formal insurance mechanisms.

*Presented at the Southern Economic Association 94th Annual Meeting (2024).*
